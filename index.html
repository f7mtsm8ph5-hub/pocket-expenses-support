Everything for a tidy expense claim

Folders for anything

A trip, a project or a month. Add expenses as you go and see the folder total at a glance.

Reads your receipts

Take or choose a photo or PDF. Pocket reads it on your phone and suggests the total, date and merchant. You tap to accept, so nothing is filled in behind your back.

Claim in one tap

Mark a folder as claimed to clear it from Active. It stays under Claimed with every receipt intact.

PDF and CSV export

Share a neat PDF with receipts, or a CSV for your accounts team, from the folder in seconds.

Many currencies

Spend in any currency. Totals convert using the rate from the day of each expense, or show every currency separately.

Personal and work cards

Record which card you used and keep personal and work totals apart.

Duplicate warnings

Pocket flags expenses that look alike across folders, so a receipt isn't claimed twice.

Mileage

Choose Car and enter miles, a rate and passengers. Pocket keeps the details with the expense.

Your expenses stay yours

On your phone only

Everything is stored on your device. We never receive your folders, expenses or receipts.

Lock it

Open Pocket with Face ID or a fingerprint, and add an optional passphrase that encrypts all your data.
