Pocket privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Pocket is an expense tracker that keeps everything on your own device. We do not collect, receive, store, sell or share any of your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What is stored on your device

The folders, expenses, amounts, descriptions, people, payment methods and receipt photos or files you add are stored only in the app's private storage on your iPhone. They are never uploaded to us. If you turn on the optional passphrase, this data is also encrypted on the device with a key made from your passphrase, which we never see. If you forget it, the data cannot be recovered.

Receipts

Receipts are read on the device using Apple's text recognition. Nothing is sent anywhere. Photos can optionally have location and device details removed before they are saved.

Face ID

If you turn on the optional lock, Pocket uses Face ID or Touch ID through Apple's system prompt. Pocket never receives your biometric data.

Network use

To convert amounts between currencies, Pocket may look up a published exchange rate. That request contains only two currency codes and a date, and it contains nothing about you or your expenses. You can turn conversion off in Settings by showing amounts in their original currencies. If you choose to import data from your own Pocket server, the app connects only to the address you type in.

Exports

PDF and CSV files you create are shared only where you choose, using the iOS share sheet. They are not encrypted once shared.

Children

Pocket does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change. Questions: see the support page.
