Pocket support

Found a bug, or need a hand? Get in touch.

Contact

Email: info@pocketexpenses.org

When reporting a problem, please include your iPhone model, your iOS version, the Pocket version, and what you did just before it went wrong. A screenshot helps. Please do not send receipts or other personal information.

Common questions

Where is my data? Only on your device. See the privacy policy.

I forgot my passphrase. It cannot be recovered or reset. The only way back in is to erase Pocket's data on the device and start again.

Face ID does not work. Your device passcode can be used instead. Check Face ID is enabled for Pocket in iOS Settings.

A receipt was read wrongly. Suggestions are never applied until you tap them. You can always edit the amount, date and description by hand.
